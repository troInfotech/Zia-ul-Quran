About the Jamia

Jamia Chishtia Razvia Zia-ul-Quran is a well-known religious institution in Rahwali, Gujranwala Cantt. Founded by Abul Ulama Hazrat Allama Maulana Muhammad Hanif Chishti (Rehmatullah Alaih), it brings together Quran memorisation, Dars-e-Nizami, formal schooling up to B.A., and computer and Artificial Intelligence (AI) education, so that students grow in both religious and worldly knowledge.

  • Founded on the Quran and Sunnah
  • Quran memorisation and Dars-e-Nizami
  • Formal education up to B.A. with computer and AI classes
  • Fitness centre for students' health and growth
